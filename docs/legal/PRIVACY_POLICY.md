## 1. Introduction

La Mia ("Discover, Cook, and Share Delicious Recipes") values your trust and is committed to protecting your personal data and privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Policy is prepared in compliance with **Republic Act No. 10173**, otherwise known as the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA)** of the Republic of the Philippines, its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and recognized global data privacy principles.

By accessing or using La Mia, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and our [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information collected automatically during app usage, and information from third-party authentication providers.

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Credentials:** When you register for an account, we collect your display name, email address, password (encrypted and hashed via Firebase Authentication), and optional profile biography or avatar image.
- **Recipe & Culinary Content:** When you create or submit a recipe, we collect the title, category, description, prep/cook times, servings, ingredient lists, step-by-step cooking instructions, and photos you upload.
- **Social & Community Interactions:** When you interact with other users, we collect your recipe likes, star ratings, comments, user follows, and reports.
- **Meal Plans & Preferences:** When you use meal planning or save favorites, we store your curated weekly schedules and saved recipes.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Recipe search queries, filtering parameters, "Cook by Ingredients" inputs, and "Ano Pong Ulam?" suggestion interactions.
- **Device & Technical Information:** Operating system version, device model, app build version, language preference, crash diagnostics, and unique installation identifiers.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens to deliver notifications regarding likes, comments, creator updates, meal plan reminders, and daily cooking suggestions (subject to your notification preferences).

### 2.3 Device Permissions Requested
La Mia requests specific system permissions only when necessary to perform app features:
- **Photo Library / Gallery (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Photo Picker):** To select and upload recipe cover photos and profile avatars.
- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Optional, to take photos directly for recipe instructions or profile pictures.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** To send cooking alerts, meal plan reminders, and social interaction notices. You can revoke this permission anytime in device settings or in-app notification preferences.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for lawful, specific, and legitimate purposes:
1. **Provide and Maintain Service:** To authenticate user accounts, host and display submitted recipes, and synchronize your meal plans and favorites across devices.
2. **Smart Culinary Features:** To calculate ingredient match percentages ("Cook by Ingredients") and provide daily meal recommendations ("Ano Pong Ulam?").
3. **Community Safety & Quality:** To moderate submitted recipes through our review queue, enforce community standards, prevent spam, and investigate reported abusive content.
4. **Communication & Notifications:** To send important security alerts, account verification emails, password reset links, and user-configured notification reminders.
5. **Analytics & Performance:** To detect app crashes, measure feature adoption, and improve app stability and user experience.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

In accordance with Section 12 of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:
- **Consent:** When you explicitly agree to these terms during account creation or grant device permissions (e.g., photo access or push notifications).
- **Contractual Necessity:** To perform our obligations in providing you with access to your account, recipes, and community features.
- **Legitimate Interests:** To protect platform security, moderate content, prevent fraud, and enhance our services without overriding your fundamental privacy rights.
- **Compliance with Legal Obligations:** When required by law enforcement or competent judicial authorities in the Philippines.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We utilize trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers that act as Data Processors:

- **Google Firebase (Alphabet Inc.):**
  - **Firebase Authentication:** Secure user identity management, credential encryption, and email verification.
  - **Cloud Firestore:** Scalable cloud database for storing user profiles, recipe documents, ratings, comments, and notifications.
  - **Firebase Cloud Storage:** Encrypted media storage for user-uploaded recipe imagery and avatars.
  - **Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics:** Anonymized diagnostic logs and performance metrics.
- **Google Sign-In:** Optional third-party federated authentication for streamlined registration.

All third-party service providers are contractually required to maintain high standards of data security and confidentiality in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Active Accounts:** We retain your personal data and User Content for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the Service.
- **Account Deletion:** You can request account deletion at any time within the app settings or by emailing our Data Protection contact. Upon deletion:
  - Your account credentials, profile details, and private meal plans will be permanently purged from our active databases.
  - User-submitted recipes that were published publicly may either be removed or anonymized (disassociating your identifying personal name and email) to preserve the integrity of community culinary collections.
- **Backups:** Residual copies may persist in encrypted system backups for up to 30 days before permanent automatic overwriting.

## 7. Your Data Subject Rights (RA 10173)

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you are entitled to the following rights as a Data Subject:

1. **Right to be Informed:** You have the right to know how your personal data is collected, handled, and processed.
2. **Right to Access:** You may request access to the personal data we hold about you.
3. **Right to Rectification:** You have the right to dispute any inaccuracy or error in your personal data and have it corrected (e.g., via the in-app Edit Profile screen).
4. **Right to Erasure or Blocking:** You may request the suspension, withdrawal, or removal of your personal data from our systems under statutory conditions.
5. **Right to Object:** You may object to the processing of your personal data, including opting out of non-essential push notifications.
6. **Right to Data Portability:** You may obtain a copy of your personal data in an electronic or structured format.
7. **Right to File a Complaint:** If you believe your data privacy rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** of the Philippines (https://privacy.gov.ph).

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, accidental loss, or destruction. These measures include:
- Industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
- Firebase database security rules restricting read and write access to authenticated users.
- Hashing and salting of password credentials.
- Periodic review of our collection and storage security practices.

While we take rigorous precautions, no wireless or digital storage method is 100% impenetrable; therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
